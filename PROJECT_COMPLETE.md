## How It Works

### Three-Layer Real-Time Sync System

**Layer 1: Periodic Refresh (Every 30s)**
- All users automatically fetch latest potholes
- Guarantees visibility within 30 seconds maximum

**Layer 2: Immediate Refresh on Upload**
- When someone uploads a pothole, all devices refresh instantly
- Potholes appear within 2-5 seconds for other users

**Layer 3: Route-Based Refresh**
- While navigating, continuously check for new potholes on route
- Priority refresh for safety-critical information
